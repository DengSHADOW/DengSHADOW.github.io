Publications & Work

PAWM: Perspective-Aware World Modeling for False-Belief Inference in Language Models in progress
Tengyang Deng
⬡ Target: ACL 2026 Workshop / COLM 2026

Large language models consistently fail on false-belief tasks not due to reasoning deficits, but because they adopt a global narrator perspective during information extraction. PAWM introduces per-agent perspective-specific belief states within a POMDP framework, restructuring how models track what each agent knows and when they knew it.

Paper (soon) Code (soon)
Curriculum Learning for Double DQN in Sparse-Reward Environments
Tengyang Deng
⬡ JHU EN.520.637 — Foundations of Reinforcement Learning

A controlled study of curriculum learning applied to Double DQN in the Flappy Bird sparse-reward environment. Key finding: DQN ≥ DDQN across all conditions; curriculum learning improved sample efficiency for vanilla DQN (reaching score 10 earliest) while paradoxically hurting DDQN performance.

Report
Proactive Interference in Large Language Models: A Cognitive Science Perspective proposal
Tengyang Deng
⬡ JHU 601.773 — Machine Social Intelligence

Project proposal exploring whether LLMs exhibit proactive interference — the phenomenon where earlier learned information disrupts retrieval of newer information — connecting memory psychology to in-context learning dynamics.
